SpeedBump Settings

Confirm List

Add domains that should require confirmation before loading. Enter one domain per line (e.g., "reddit.com", "twitter.com"). The extension will match the exact domain and all subdomains.

Examples

  • reddit.com - Matches reddit.com, www.reddit.com, old.reddit.com, etc.
  • youtube.com - Matches youtube.com, www.youtube.com, m.youtube.com, etc.
  • twitter.com - Matches twitter.com and all Twitter subdomains
